Your next passport stamp starts in Kathmandu.

We guide travellers from Kathmandu through visit-visa applications for Canada, the United Kingdom, Australia, New Zealand, the Schengen countries, Korea, China and Japan — counseling, documentation, itineraries and the embassy process, handled end to end.

A travel consultancy built on document precision.

Canon Travel & Tours is based in Kathmandu and has guided more than 5,000 clients through the visit-visa process — from the first counseling conversation about their finances, travel history and ties to Nepal, through to the day their passport comes back stamped.

Every application is different. Our work is figuring out, case by case, which documents make a profile strong, and which are simply unnecessary.

From first conversation to stamped passport.

Six stages of work sit behind every visit-visa file we submit.

01

Visit-visa counseling

We assess income, travel history and ties to Nepal, then advise honestly on which destination and visa class actually fits the client's profile.

02

Document preparation

Personal, income and property documents, collected, translated where needed, and sorted into a single client folder.

03

Itinerary & reservations

Day-by-day travel plans, refundable hotel bookings and flight reservations that match the dates on the application.

04

Cover letter drafting

The two-to-three page narrative that ties finances, itinerary and ties-to-Nepal into one document for the visa officer.

05

Portal, biometrics & tracking

Embassy portal registration, form submission, VFS or TLS biometric appointments, and status tracking through to the final decision.

How a file moves from Kathmandu to a decision.

Seven stops, the same route for every client.

  1. 1

    Counseling & profile review

    We assess finances, travel history and ties to Nepal to determine whether a profile meets the target country's criteria.

  2. 2

    Document collection & folder sorting

    Personal, income, tax and property documents are gathered, translated where required, and organised into one client folder.

  3. 3

    Itinerary & reservations

    A day-by-day travel plan is drafted, with refundable hotel bookings and a flight reservation to match.

  4. 4

    Cover letter drafting

    Finances, itinerary and ties to Nepal are written into a single narrative for the visa officer.

  5. 5

    Portal registration & submission

    An account is created on the relevant embassy portal, forms are filled out, and documents are uploaded.

  6. 6

    Biometrics appointment

    A slot is booked at VFS, TLS or the equivalent centre for fingerprint and photo capture.

  7. 7

    Decision & passport collection

    We track the application status and collect the stamped passport once a decision is made.
